# CV Checklist Instructions

## Scope
⚠️ Apply these steps only after completing the LinkedIn checklist for content and text.

---

## 🧩 Format

- Use a proper template.
- US companies prefer LaTeX-style CVs.
- Use the **"silver-dev-cv" template in Typst**.
- Avoid creative designs or Word-based resumes — they reduce perceived quality and may lead to rejection.
- Keep the CV to **one page only**.

---

## 📄 Main Content

- Your CV must be **consistent with your LinkedIn profile**, but not identical.
  - Any discrepancy can create doubt, and doubt leads to rejection.
  
- **Tailor your CV for each company**:
  - Review LinkedIn profiles of people working at the target company.
  - Use them as benchmarks (“winners”).
  - Adjust:
    - Job titles
    - Descriptions
    - Messaging
    - Skills
  - Align your CV with what the company is looking for.

- Tell a **clear story** that highlights your strongest points.

- ✅ Recommended:
  - Add a short **introduction / summary section** tailored per company.
  - It should explicitly or implicitly answer:
    > “Why should Company X hire me?”

- Do **not include images or a profile photo**.
  - This is considered inappropriate for US-based applications.

- After every edit:
  - Run the CV through **Grammarly**.
  - Typos or grammar mistakes are unacceptable.

---

## ❌ What NOT to Do

- Do not create your own templates.
- Do not use outdated tools like Microsoft Word.
- Avoid “spray & pray” strategies (sending the same generic CV everywhere).
- Do not include images or photos.
- Do not exceed one page.
- Do not use email addresses like:
  - `@hotmail`
  - `@outlook`
- Prefer a **Gmail address** (you can set up forwarding if needed).

- Do not use:
  - Star ratings
  - Progress bars
  - Skill percentages  
  to represent your level of expertise.

- Do not include a GitHub link if:
  - You don’t have meaningful projects
  - You lack significant contributions or activity

---

## Goal

Produce a **focused, high-signal, tailored CV** that:
- Matches your LinkedIn narrative
- Aligns with the target company
- Clearly communicates your value
- Minimizes any potential doubts
